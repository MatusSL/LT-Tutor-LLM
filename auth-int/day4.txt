Day 4 — Thread user_id Through the Backend                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                    
  The ripple effect:
                                                                                                                                                                    
  Once get_current_user works, every route handler can access the user. But the services downstream don't know about it yet.                                        
   
  Currently vocabulary.py:61 does .eq(User.DISPLAY_NAME, "matus"). That needs to become .eq("user_id", user.id) — and the user_id has to flow from the route →      
  through TutorCore → into Vocabulary.                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                    
  What changes:                                                                                                                                                   

  1. SessionState gets a user_id field                                                                                                                              
  2. Vocabulary methods accept user_id as a parameter (or receive it at construction)
  3. TutorCore.handle_message() receives the user and passes it down                                                                                                
  4. All .eq(User.DISPLAY_NAME, "matus") calls become .eq("user_id", user_id)                                                                                       
  5. Same for words and mistakes queries — always filter by user_id                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                                    
  Task for Day 4:                                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                    
  1. Thread user_id through the call chain                                                                                                                          
  2. Remove every hardcoded "matus" reference
